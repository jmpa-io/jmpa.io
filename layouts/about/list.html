Hi, I'm Jordan.

Programmer by day. Watercolourist by night.

I'm a software engineer based in Melbourne, Australia. I've been making watercolour paintings and ink drawings for years — mostly landscapes, urban sketches, and loose florals inspired by wherever I happen to be.

Everything on this site is original and one of a kind. No prints, no editions.

About the work

Most pieces are small-format — 15×15cm up to 30×21cm — painted on cold press watercolour paper. I work mainly with transparent watercolour, occasionally with gouache or ink. Subject matter leans toward places: Melbourne streets, the Yarra, the Victorian coast.

If it's listed, it's the original. If it's sold, it's gone.

Commissions

I take a limited number of commissions each year — location pieces, landscapes, loose botanicals, and pet portraits. Read more about the process here or get in touch directly.

Questions about a piece, shipping, or a commission enquiry — email is best.
