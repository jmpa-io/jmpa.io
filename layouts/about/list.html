Hi, I'm Jordan.

Programmer by day. Watercolourist by night.

I'm a software engineer based in Melbourne, Australia. I've been making watercolour paintings and ink drawings for years - mostly landscapes, urban sketches, and loose florals inspired by wherever I happen to be travelling.

The work on this site is for sale. Everything is original and one of a kind - no prints. Payments are handled by Square and each piece ships from Melbourne.

About the work

Most pieces are small-format - 15×15cm up to 30×21cm - painted on cold press watercolour paper or toned paper. I work mainly with transparent watercolour, occasionally with gouache or ink. The subject matter leans toward places: Melbourne streets, the Yarra, the Victorian coast, wherever I've been with a sketchbook.

I don't do editions or reproductions. If it's listed, it's the original. If it's sold, it's gone.

Commissions

I take a limited number of commissions each year - mostly location pieces, portraits, and pet portraits. If you have something specific in mind, read more about the process here or get in touch directly.

Get in touch

Questions about a piece, shipping, or a commission enquiry - email is the best way to reach me.
