Privacy.

How jmpa.io handles your information. Short version: very little is collected and none of it is sold.

What we collect

If you make a purchase or enquiry, we collect the information you provide — name, email, and shipping address. This is used only to fulfil your order or respond to your message.

We don't run analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party ad scripts. No cookies are set by this site beyond what's needed for the shopping cart (stored locally in your browser).

Payments

Payments are processed by Square. Your card details go directly to Square — jmpa.io never sees or stores them. Square's privacy policy governs how they handle that data.

Email

If you email us at support@jmpa.io, we keep that correspondence to handle your query. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Sharing

We don't sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except where required to fulfil an order (e.g. providing a shipping address to Australia Post).

Your rights

You can request to see, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you. Email us and we'll respond promptly.
