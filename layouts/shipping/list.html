Packaging & delivery

Shipping.

All original work ships from Melbourne, Australia.

Packaging

Every piece is packed with acid-free backing board and wrapped in glassine before going into a rigid mailer or cardboard tube. Nothing arrives bent.

Domestic (Australia)

Flat rate of $12 AUD via Australia Post tracked parcel. Typically 3–7 business days. Express available on request — email before purchasing if you need it urgently.

International

I ship internationally. Costs vary by destination — email me your location and the piece you're interested in and I'll give you an exact quote before you commit.

International buyers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged by their country's customs authority.

Returns

Because each piece is an original one-of-a-kind work, I don't accept returns for change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and damage and email me within 7 days — I'll sort it out.
