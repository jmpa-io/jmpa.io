Shipping & Returns

All original work ships from Melbourne, Australia.

Packaging

Every piece is packaged with acid-free backing board and wrapped in glassine before going into a rigid mailer or cardboard tube. Nothing arrives bent.

Domestic shipping (Australia)

Flat rate of $12 AUD via Australia Post tracked parcel. Delivery is typically 3-7 business days depending on your location. Express post is available on request for an additional cost - email me before purchasing if you need it urgently.

International shipping

I ship internationally. Costs vary by destination - email me with your location and the piece you're interested in and I'll give you an exact shipping quote before you commit to purchasing.

Please note: international buyers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged by their country's customs authority.

Returns

Because each piece is an original one-of-a-kind work, I don't accept returns for change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, take a photo of the packaging and the damage and email me within 7 days - I'll sort it out.
