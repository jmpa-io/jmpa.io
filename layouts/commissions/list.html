Commissions

Custom original work, made to order.

What I take on

I accept a small number of commission requests each year. Work I enjoy doing on commission: location pieces (a house, a favourite place, a travel memory), landscapes, loose botanical studies, and pet portraits. I don't do photorealistic portraiture or digital work.

The process

Send me an email with a brief description of what you have in mind - subject, rough size, any reference photos. I'll come back to you within a few days with whether I can take it on, an indicative price, and a rough timeline.

Once we agree on the details, I ask for a 50% deposit upfront. I'll send a photo of the finished piece before shipping. Final payment is due before I post it out.

Pricing

Commission pricing depends on size and complexity. As a rough guide:

  • Small (up to 15×15cm) - from $40 AUD
  • Medium (up to 21×30cm) - from $80 AUD
  • Large (30cm+) - from $150 AUD

Shipping is additional. See the shipping & returns page for details.

Turnaround

Typically 2-4 weeks from deposit to postage, depending on my current queue. I'll give you a realistic estimate when we agree on the work.
