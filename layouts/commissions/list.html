Custom original work

Commissions.

Made to order, one at a time. A small number of spots open each year.

What I take on

Location pieces, landscapes, loose botanical studies, and pet portraits. I don't do photorealistic portraiture or digital work.

The process

Send me an email with a brief description of what you have in mind — subject, rough size, any reference photos. I'll come back to you within a few days with whether I can take it on, a price, and a timeline.

Once agreed, I ask for a 50% deposit upfront. I'll send a photo of the finished piece before shipping. Final payment is due before I post it.

Pricing

Depends on size and complexity. As a rough guide:

  • Small (up to 15×15cm) — from $40 AUD
  • Medium (up to 21×30cm) — from $80 AUD
  • Large (30cm+) — from $150 AUD

Shipping is additional. See the shipping page for details.

Turnaround

Typically 2–4 weeks from deposit to postage, depending on the current queue.
